Fines & Library Policies

Late Returns

To ensure books are available for all members, we enforce late fines for items not returned by their due date.

Current Fine Rate: {{ request.organization.currency|default:"$" }}{{ request.organization.settings.fine_per_day|default:"5.00" }} per day, per overdue item.

Fines are calculated automatically each day an item is overdue. If your total fines exceed a certain threshold, your borrowing privileges may be temporarily suspended until the balance is paid.

Lost or Damaged Items

Members are responsible for the condition of the materials they borrow.

  • Damaged items: Fines will be assessed based on the severity of the damage, up to the full replacement cost.
  • Lost items: You will be charged the replacement cost of the book, plus a processing fee. Overdue fines already accrued on the item will also apply.

How to Pay Fines

You can view your outstanding fines by navigating to the Fines section from the sidebar. Currently, payments must be made in-person at the library circulation desk. Please speak to a staff member to clear your balance.
