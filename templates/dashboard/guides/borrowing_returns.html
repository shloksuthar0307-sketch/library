Borrowing & Returns Guide

How to Borrow a Book

Borrowing physical books from the library is a straightforward process. As a member, you can browse the catalog and reserve items ahead of time, or check them out directly at the circulation desk.

  • Find the book you want in the Books catalog.
  • If it is available, you can click Reserve to hold it for pickup.
  • Visit the library branch and present your Member ID (found on your profile).
  • The staff will issue the book to your account, and you'll receive a due date.

Returning Books

Books must be returned by their due date to avoid late fines. You can return a book to any branch within your organization unless specified otherwise.

Renewals

Need more time? If a book hasn't been reserved by another member, you may be able to renew it. Navigate to your active transactions on the Dashboard and click Renew if the option is available.
