Getting Started with SmartLibrary

Welcome to SmartLibrary

SmartLibrary is your all-in-one platform for managing your library experience. Whether you are a member looking to borrow a book, or an administrator managing thousands of titles across multiple branches, this guide will help you get started.

Setting up your profile

  1. Click on your avatar in the top right corner of the dashboard.
  2. Select My Profile from the dropdown menu.
  3. Update your personal information, contact details, and preferences.
  4. If you need to change your password, you can do so securely from this page.

Navigating the Dashboard

The sidebar on the left is your main navigation hub. Depending on your role, you will see different options:

  • Dashboard: Your personalized home screen with quick stats and recent activity.
  • Books & Categories: Browse the library catalog, see what's available, and read summaries.
  • Reservations: View and manage books you have put on hold.
  • Fines: Check if you have any outstanding balances for overdue items.

Pro Tip: Use the search bar at the top of the Books page to quickly find titles by author, ISBN, or keywords!
